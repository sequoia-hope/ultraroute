Where it stands

The router is our own MIT code in core/: a grid router with negotiated congestion, fan-out, shoving and a via pass, run as one fixed recipe. It reads and writes KiCad boards and follows KiCad's own rules for clearances, slots, net ties and keepouts.

On a 200-board sample of PCBench it leaves 164 boards with no DRC errors, a median board in about nine seconds on one thread. Runs are repeatable byte for byte, and memory is estimated before routing and kept under a cap.

Next: fewer vias, and boards that are unfinishable today. The roadmap has the plan.
